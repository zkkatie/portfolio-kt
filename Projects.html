Mr. Bones

You play as Noah Bones, a soul trapped in hell who can shrink and expand to get through the level, collect items and find a way out. It runs on Titan Engine, which my team wrote from scratch instead of using Unity or Unreal. I handle resource management, asset management and audio.

Year 1, Trimester 2 · Team project

Don't Touch My Grass!

You're a farmer keeping animals off your patch of grass. Every level the patch gets bigger and more animals show up. Built in C++ on AE Engine.

  • C++
  • AE Engine
  • Gameplay
  • Sprites
View full projectHide details

What I did

  • The five animal enemies (worm, crow, horse, cow, goat): sprites, movement speeds, spawning and draw order
  • Powerup orbs that boost speed, boost damage or freeze enemies, dropped when an enemy dies
  • Main menu, settings and credits screens, and the transitions between levels

How it plays

  • Animals walk in from the edges, heading for your grass
  • Each animal moves at its own speed
  • Three levels, each with a bigger patch to protect

DoNut Panic

Slither.io, but you're a donut. Eat smaller donuts to grow, avoid the bad ones, and get big enough to eat the boss donut before it eats you. Written in C with Cprocessing.

  • C
  • Cprocessing
  • Gameplay
View full projectHide details

What I did

  • Designed the three donut variants
  • Built the growth mechanic: eat prey, earn points, get bigger

How it plays

  • Eating a "bad" donut costs health
  • Touching a bigger donut ends the run
  • Outgrow the boss donut and eat it before time runs out

Tabletop game · Team project

Bubbles' Delivery

A solo tabletop game where you play Bubbles, an axolotl courier trying to earn enough to pay off a debt before he loses his scooter. The board is 14 tiles with currents, weather and encounter cards. I designed "Flip of Fate", one of the DLC tiles, which pairs a coin flip with wind, water and mud effects.
