About me

I'm Katie, a Computer Science student specialising in Interactive Media and Game Design. Most of what I've made so far are team games: a slither.io-style donut game in C, a farm defence game in C++, and now Mr. Bones, which runs on an engine my team wrote ourselves.

On those teams I've mostly worked on gameplay, like enemies, powerups and menus, and on the systems underneath, like resource and asset management and audio. I'm looking for internships and projects where I can keep doing both.
