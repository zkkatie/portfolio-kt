One Million Quest

Under a city shophouse, Mr. Doostbin runs an underground Labubu reselling den: a counterfeit factory, a casino for laundering money, a guard wing and a livestream studio, all linked by vents. You play Bobby, a worker lured in by a fake job offer, who has to collect 1,000,000 Labubu Coins of evidence across 8 zones and escape before Mr. Doostbin catches him.

What I designed

  • 16 key events over three phases, each worth a set number of Labubu Coins
  • A coin economy where 1,000,000 clears the level and optional events push it to 1,150,000

Mega Man Maker · Solo project · 3 levels

Overgrowth

Escape the overgrown lab. A research facility has been taken over by weeds, and you have to fight your way out across three levels: Easy, Normal and Hard. Each one is built screen by screen around a cadence: introduce a mechanic on its own, stack it with the others towards a peak, then ease off before the finish.

  • Mega Man Maker
  • Level design
  • Cadence & pacing
  • Difficulty tuning
View full projectHide details

Mechanic legend

  • A Conveyor belts that pull you left or right
  • B Springs to bounce up to platforms or across gaps
  • C Swings: grab the bulb, ride across, let go on time
  • D Spikes and the fireball-throwing enemy
  • E Falling blocks that drop soon after you land

How the levels are paced

  • Easy teaches one mechanic per screen, then combines conveyors, springs and spikes to peak on screen 6
  • Normal introduces swings with safe platforms below if you miss, builds to all four mechanics on screen 6, then teaches a harder swing move before the finale
  • Reused 4-koma rooms come back with a new tileset; in Easy, one gives a second, easier pass right after the peak
  • Hard adds falling blocks, hides an optional bonus room behind the advanced swing, and ends with a recharge before the boss

Game Design

Solo tabletop game · Team project

Bubbles' Delivery

Bubbles the axolotl took a shady loan to fix his scooter and now owes 80 shellbits. You have 8 deliveries to earn it back on a 14-tile lakeside board, planning routes through water, land and currents with each dice roll.

  • Tabletop design
  • Systems design
  • Figma
View the Figma board ↗
View full projectHide details

How it plays

  • At a post office, draw 3 delivery tokens and pick 1; each lists 3 drop-offs, a dice budget and a reward
  • Roll 2d6 for movement: water costs 1, land costs 2, and riding a current covers 2 steps for 1
  • Deliver all 3 packages and get back to a post office before your dice run out, or the delivery fails
  • Encounter points draw cards that can help or hurt, including weather that lasts the whole delivery

Design goals

  • Turn real courier problems, like tight deadlines and route planning, into the core loop
  • Higher-risk deliveries pay more, so every pick is a gamble against your dice
  • Bubbles is an axolotl, so water and rain help him while land and heat slow him down
  • I designed "Flip of Fate", one of the DLC tiles, which pairs a coin flip with wind, water and mud effects
